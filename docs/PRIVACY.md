# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 1 October 2026**

PocketCFO helps you track your own money. That only works if you trust it with real
numbers, so this page explains exactly what we hold, what we never look at, and how
to get rid of all of it.

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We have no advertising partners.

---

## What you give us

**Your account.** An email address, and a password if you did not sign in with
Google. If you use Google sign-in, we receive your email address from Google and
nothing else.

**Your financial information.** Everything you enter in the app:

- income and payday dates
- bills and subscriptions
- transactions you log
- debts and their balances
- savings vaults and their targets
- your reconciliation history

This is stored in our database so the app works across your devices. It is tied to
your account and is visible only to you.

**Your PIN or fingerprint.** If you lock the app, that stays on your device. It is
never sent to us. We cannot see it and cannot recover it for you.

---

## What we measure

We record how people move through the app so we can tell where it confuses them.
This is deliberately limited:

- which screens are opened
- how far you get through setup, and where you stop
- whether you viewed or clicked an upgrade prompt
- crashes and errors, so we can fix them

**We do not send your financial figures to our analytics.** Your Safe-to-Spend
number, your balances and your bill totals are removed before any usage data leaves
your device. The analytics can tell us that someone stopped at the bills step. It
cannot tell us what their bills are.

**We do not record your screen.** Some apps use session replay, which captures video
of what you do. We have switched it off. Given what this app displays, we do not
think it belongs here.

Usage data is tied to a browser or device, not to your account. We do not link it to
your name or email.

---

## Who else is involved

We use a small number of outside services to run PocketCFO. Each one sees only what
it needs.

| Service | What it does | What it sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase | Stores your account and financial data | Your email and everything you enter |
| PostHog | Usage and crash reporting | Screens visited, setup progress, errors. No amounts |
| Vercel | Hosts the app, measures traffic and speed | Page requests and performance |
| LemonSqueezy | Handles payments | Your payment details, which we never receive |

Card details go straight to LemonSqueezy. They never reach us and we cannot see them.

Your data is held in the United States. Your account and financial information sit in
Supabase's US East region, and our usage data is held by PostHog in the United
States. If you use PocketCFO from outside the US, your information is transferred
there so the service can work.

---

## How long we keep it

Your account data is kept until you delete your account. Usage data is kept for as
long as our analytics provider retains it, currently about a year.

---

## Deleting everything

You can delete your account from Settings at any time. This removes your account and
every record attached to it: transactions, bills, debts, vaults, subscriptions and
history. If you have a paid subscription, it is cancelled first.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. We do not keep a copy.

---

## Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, or delete it.
The fastest route for all three is the app itself: your data is visible in the app,
editable in the app, and deletable from Settings.

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, the UK GDPR and GDPR give you
these rights formally, along with the right to complain to your data protection
authority. Our basis for holding your financial data is that we cannot provide the
service without it. Our basis for usage measurement is our legitimate interest in
knowing which parts of the app do not work.

If you are in South Africa, POPIA gives you equivalent rights, and you may complain
to the Information Regulator.
